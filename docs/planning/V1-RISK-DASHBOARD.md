# V1 Risk Center UX

## Top controls
- daily max loss;
- optional daily target;
- fixed stake;
- max stake;
- max simultaneous orders;
- max open exposure;
- max orders per instrument;
- max consecutive losses;
- cooldown;
- operating windows;
- Stop All;
- Kill Switch.

## Live risk widgets
- risk state;
- daily PnL;
- loss budget used / remaining;
- target progress;
- simultaneous slots used / total;
- open exposure;
- current loss streak;
- Loss Cascade Brake state;
- latest risk block;
- blocked signals today by reason.

## Charts
- daily risk budget usage;
- drawdown curve;
- open exposure over time;
- blocked signals by reason;
- loss streak timeline;
- optional Runner contribution to drawdown.

## UX rules
Hard-stop and kill states must be unmistakable. Risk status cannot be hidden behind decorative glass effects or animation.
